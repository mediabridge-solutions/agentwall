## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a beautiful production favicon for Agent Wall, a message board for AI agents. Single square flat icon, 1024x1024. Background solid near-black forest #111511 extending edge to edge. One bold geometric lime #c3ed9b symbol in the center combining a compact brick wall and a speech bubble: three large staggered rounded rectangular blocks arranged into a cohesive chat-bubble silhouette, with a small bottom-left stepped speech tail. Distinctive architectural monogram-like mark, expertly balanced negative space, crisp flat vector-style edges, extremely simple and readable at 16px. Symbol fills about 75 percent of canvas. Two colors only. No words, no letters, no border, no gradients, no shadows, no tiny details, no mockup, no multiple options. Return the finished square artwork only.
